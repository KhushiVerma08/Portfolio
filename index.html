Methodology & Thinking

A good post starts before the design.

My step-by-step thinking process for turning community initiatives and ideas into purposeful digital storytelling.

STEP 01

Understand the Core Message

“What are we trying to communicate, and why does this matter?”

✦

Define the Objective: Is it to educate, mobilize participants, recruit, or celebrate a milestone?

✦

The Core Takeaway: What is the single sentence we want people to remember?

✦

Dignity & Authenticity: Does the narrative honor the community and event context?

STEP 02

Identify the Target Audience

“Who needs to see it, and why would they care while scrolling?”

✦

Audience Persona: College students, volunteers, educators, or community partners?

✦

The Hook: What shared feeling or question will make them stop scrolling?

✦

Language Simplicity: Avoid heavy jargon; use approachable words.

STEP 03

Structure the Story

“What is the simplest and strongest way to tell it?”

✦

Carousel Flow: Strong Hook → The Context/Challenge → Concrete Insights → Call to Action.

✦

Human Voices: Ground abstract topics in real quotes and volunteer stories.

✦

Emotional Tone: Warm, inspiring, and actionable.

STEP 04

Design with Visual Hierarchy

“Create a visual hierarchy that makes the message easy to understand.”

✦

Typography & Contrast: Clear headlines paired with legible body text.

✦

White Space: Avoid clutter; let each slide focus on one key idea.

✦

Canva & Figma Craft: Consistent color palette, clean alignments, and intentional visual cues.

STEP 05

Adapt & Publish Across Platforms

“Adapt the creative for Instagram, LinkedIn, or Stories.”

✦

Instagram Carousels (4:5): Multi-slide value guides for saves and shares.

✦

Stories (9:16): Quick interactive polls, event countdowns, and behind-the-scenes clips.

✦

LinkedIn: Professional takeaways, event recaps, and collaboration updates.

STEP 06

Learn & Iterate

“Look at reach, engagement, saves, and audience response.”

✦

Engagement Quality: Are people saving the post, sharing with friends, or asking questions in comments?

✦

Slide Drop-off: Did readers swipe to the final slide?

✦

Refine: Use audience feedback to make the next campaign even better.

✦ “I’m still learning—but I’m curious about every step of the process.”

Data Science Meets Storytelling

Analytics Mindset: Create → Publish → Measure → Learn → Improve

Understanding social media beyond aesthetics through key qualitative and quantitative metrics:

🔖

Saves (High Utility)

Shows that educational content was valuable enough for people to refer back to.

↗️

Shares (Advocacy)

Indicates the story resonated enough to be passed on to friends and communities.

💬

Comments (Dialogue)

Measures true community engagement, feedback, and thoughtful conversations.

📊

Reach & Non-Followers

Evaluates how effectively the topic and visual hook expanded beyond our current circle.

⏱️

Carousel Completion Rate

Tracks retention across slides to understand where audience attention holds or drops.

🌱

Community Quality

Focusing on attracting genuine participants and collaborators rather than empty vanity numbers.
